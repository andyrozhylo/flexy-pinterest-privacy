Privacy Policy for Flexy Visual Research Connector

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy describes the planned personal-use connector. It does not collect Pinterest account data until the account holder authorizes it and the connector is enabled.

Who we are

Flexy is responsible for this connector. Contact us at a.rozhylo@gmail.com. Our website is flexyplanner.com.

What the connector does

The connector is intended to let the Pinterest account holder authorize read-only access to their own boards and Pins and request them in a private research interface. Access to secret boards or Pins will be requested only if the account holder enables that permission. The connector will not create Pins, change boards, manage ads, or access other people's private accounts.

Information we process

After Pinterest OAuth authorization, the connector may receive an account identifier, board identifiers and names, Pin identifiers, images, links, descriptions, and other metadata returned by the approved Pinterest API for the account holder's requests. It may also process OAuth access and refresh tokens and minimal technical records needed to diagnose errors and protect the service. We will not ask for a Pinterest password.

How we use information

Pinterest information will be used to retrieve and display requested boards and Pins, filter the authorized account's content by keyword, and answer the account holder's requests. If an AI connector is approved and enabled, the specific results requested inside ChatGPT will be sent to OpenAI to generate a response. This feature will not be enabled unless the transfer is permitted under Pinterest's developer rules. Flexy will not sell Pinterest information, use it for advertising, or use Pinterest material to train or improve AI models.

Storage and retention

The connector is designed to fetch board and Pin information from Pinterest when needed, without maintaining a permanent copy of Pinterest content. OAuth credentials will be kept only while the account remains connected and protected by access controls. Technical records will be kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting. If an AI feature is enabled, the AI service may retain the conversation under its own terms and account settings.

Your choices and deletion

The account holder can revoke access in Pinterest settings. They can also email Flexy to request disconnection and deletion of connector-held credentials and technical records. Revoking access stops new API requests. It does not delete information held by Pinterest or a separate AI service under that service's own terms.

Service providers and security

Pinterest provides the account data. A hosting provider may process credentials and technical records to operate the connector. OpenAI will process only requested results sent through an enabled ChatGPT integration. Flexy will use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect credentials.

Children and changes

This connector is intended for the authorized account holder and is not directed to children. We may update this policy when the connector or its data practices change. A revised version will be published at this URL before materially different processing begins.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email a.rozhylo@gmail.com.
